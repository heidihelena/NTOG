Nordic Lung Cancer Quality Indicator Bank

A curated bank of candidate quality indicators for the Nordic lung cancer diagnostic pathway. These are proposals put forward by NTOG for the Nordic community to prioritise — rating and discussion take place on LinkedIn. Agreed indicators will feed the NTOG Nordic common data elements.

Candidate indicators — not finalised. This bank is a working proposal for community discussion. Nothing here is an endorsed NTOG quality standard yet; the list will evolve as the Nordic community weighs in.
