13 to 15 May 2027 · Scandic Copenhagen, Denmark

Scientific Programme

Preliminary programme for the 16th Nordic Lung Cancer Symposium: plenary sessions on screening, early-stage, locally advanced and stage IV NSCLC, Nordic trial collaboration, and parallel tracks on Friday and Saturday.

Call for abstracts Back to symposium page
Preliminary programme

Three days in Copenhagen

Sessions, times and titles may change. Speakers will be added as they are confirmed.

Venue: Scandic Copenhagen, Vester Søgade 6, Copenhagen, Denmark. Abstract submission is open until 19 March 2027 – selected abstracts are presented on Saturday.

Thursday 13 May Friday 14 May Saturday 15 May
