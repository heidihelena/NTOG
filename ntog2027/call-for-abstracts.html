13 to 15 May 2027 · Scandic Copenhagen, Denmark

Call for Abstracts

Share your work with the Nordic thoracic oncology community at the 16th Nordic Lung Cancer Symposium. We welcome clinical, translational, screening, surgical, radiation, nursing and patient-partnered research, including work in progress.

Submit your abstract How it works

Submission is open. Deadline: 19 March 2027, 23:59 CET. One short online form and one 16:9 slide by email. No login.

How to submit

Three steps

1

Write your abstract

Structured: Background · Methods · Results · Conclusions. Max 300 words, in English. Text only: no tables or figures.

2

Submit the form

Paste it into the online form. One page, about five minutes, no login. You get a copy of your submission by email.

3

Send your digital abstract

Required: one 16:9 slide that summarises your work (PDF or PNG, max 10 MB). Email it to abstract@ntog.org by the submission deadline, with your abstract title as the subject. Digital abstracts of accepted work are published on 16 April 2027.

Download 16:9 template (.pptx)

Key dates

  • Submission opensOpen now
  • Submission deadline19 March 2027, 23:59 CET
  • Scientific committee review20 March – 15 April 2027
  • Notification of acceptance16 April 2027

Good to know

  • Work in progress is welcome.
  • Previously presented research is welcome, provided you disclose where it was presented.
  • Oral presentations are selected from the submitted abstracts.
  • Accepted abstracts appear in the digital abstract book, built from your form answers and reproduced as submitted. The scientific committee is not responsible for the content of individual abstracts.

Topics

All thoracic oncology is welcome: non-small cell and small cell lung cancer, mesothelioma, thymic tumours and other thoracic malignancies.

  • Prevention, screening & early detection
  • Diagnostics, pathology & biomarkers
  • Surgery
  • Radiotherapy
  • Systemic therapy
  • Supportive & palliative care, survivorship
  • Nursing & allied health
  • Epidemiology, registries & real-world evidence
  • Health services, quality & equity
  • AI, digital health & health technology
  • Patient-partnered research
  • Education & training
If your abstract is accepted

How to present in Copenhagen

Authors are notified by 16 April 2027. You prepare your poster or talk yourself before the symposium in May, with your own template or ours.

Printed poster

A1 (594 × 841 mm), portrait or landscape. The poster boards take at most 86 cm wide × 117 cm high, so A0 does not fit. Print it yourself and bring it to Copenhagen. Our template is portrait.

Download A1 poster template (.pptx)

Oral presentation

For abstracts selected for oral presentation. Slides in 16:9. The template has nine slides, from title and disclosures to key numbers and conclusions.

Download talk slide template (.pptx)

Our templates are editable in PowerPoint, Keynote or Google Slides. They use the fonts Montserrat & Open Sans. Install them (free) for an exact match, or PowerPoint will substitute similar fonts.
